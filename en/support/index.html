Common questions

Where can I download Unfold?+

Unfold 1.0.0 is being prepared for the Mac App Store. Public downloads are not available yet. This page will include the store link after release.

Why did a reminder not appear?+

Check automatic reminders, pause status, quiet hours, and any Focus, calendar, or app avoidance rules. Notification-only reminders need system notification permission. Unfold stays in the menu bar after you close the main window.

How do I restore Pro?+

Open Settings → General → Unfold Pro → Restore Purchases using the original purchasing Apple account. If the product is temporarily unavailable, check your network and store account, then retry. Request refunds through Apple purchase support.

Are monthly statistics free?+

Yes, including earlier months. Daily and weekly details show the last seven days for free. Pro unlocks earlier daily and weekly details. Changes to Pro access never delete history.

How do I manage history?+

In Settings → General, turn recording off, export history, or delete records. Disabling recording keeps existing history. Re-enabling it records only future activity and does not recreate missing data.

Which Mac and permissions do I need?+

An Apple silicon Mac running macOS 15 or later. Notification permission is used for notifications; calendar access is requested only when you enable calendar avoidance. Unfold does not need Accessibility or Screen Recording permission.
