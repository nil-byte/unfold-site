Privacy policy

Applies to Unfold 1.0.0 · Updated October 13, 2026

01. Local data

Unfold does not upload usage activity or break history to the developer and does not read screen contents. Optional history stays on your Mac. It can include derived usage and away intervals, timed breaks, reminder attempts, and their times and outcomes. Missing activity is not reconstructed while the app is closed, recording is disabled, or facts are unknown.

02. Settings and optional permissions

Reminder settings, appearance, language, avoidance rules, messages, and imported backgrounds stay locally. Notifications deliver reminders. Calendar access requires explicit permission and selected scope, and is used only to avoid busy events. Unfold neither edits events nor stores their titles, participants, or contents. App avoidance identifies selected apps without saving activity details to history. Focus filters postpone reminders. Revoke permissions in System Settings.

03. Purchases

Apple StoreKit handles purchases and restores. Unfold uses verified transactions to determine Pro access and does not receive card details or payment passwords. Apple account, payment, and store services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

04. Control and deletion

Disable recording while keeping existing history. Export or delete records in Settings, remove backgrounds, and disable optional rules. You choose where exported files are stored or shared.

05. Support email

If you contact support, email services process the address and content you provide. Send only what is needed to investigate, and avoid calendar contents, payment details, or other private information.

06. Website and third parties

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process access information under its privacy policy. The pages contain no analytics or advertising scripts, do not set tracking cookies, and use no third-party fonts. Local app activity is not sent to this website.
